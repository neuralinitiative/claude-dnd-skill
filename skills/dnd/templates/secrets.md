# Knowledge Registry — <campaign>

*DM-only. Who in the world knows each piece of sensitive information. Everything in the campaign files is
known to the DM; this file says who **in the fiction** knows it. An NPC only states, uses or reacts to an
entry if it is listed under **Known by** (or hints at it if under **Suspected by**). See `SKILL-secrets.md`.*

*Entry ids are short slugs (`heonn-true-name`, `party-plan-ambush`). Kinds: `pc` (a PC's hidden identity,
nature, past or allegiance), `party` (a plan, a deal or a fact the party keeps private), `quest`, `npc`,
`faction`, `world`. "How" is one of: saw, told (by whom), overheard, deduced, network (which one), document.*
